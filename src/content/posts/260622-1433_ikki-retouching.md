## 💡 3 Core Principles for Next-Level Webtoon Retouching
Instead of memorizing endless formulas, mastering the logic behind retouching is key. Illustrator Ikki breaks down how to inject that professional quality into your webtoons by focusing on understanding the 'why' behind every edit.

### 1. Mastering the Art of Focal Points and Visual Flow
It's not just about adding effects; it's about guiding the viewer's eye. Understanding how to use color and lighting to establish clear focal points for your subjects is paramount. This involves strategic hue design, creating effective color keys and scripts that align with your story's intent, and then using lighting techniques—from high-key to abstract—to naturally draw attention where it matters most, ensuring the scroll direction enhances the narrative, not hinders it.

### 2. Streamlining Your Workflow with Objective Evaluation
Achieving peak work efficiency means developing a systematic retouching process. This involves creating your own retouching instructions or plans, essentially drawing editing storyboards for different themes. By using a well-designed checklist and understanding objective feedback, you can design a retouching process that is not only fast but also consistently delivers superb quality, moving beyond subjective preferences.

### 3. The Power of Intent-Driven Lighting and Color Harmony
Truly professional webtoons don't just have good colors; they have colors and lighting that serve the scene's emotional core. Learn to design lighting that perfectly suits the intention of your editing, whether it’s to create a dramatic mood or a soft, inviting atmosphere. This also extends to grouping elements thoughtfully, considering how they'll appear in sequence and how the overall color balance communicates the intended feeling.
