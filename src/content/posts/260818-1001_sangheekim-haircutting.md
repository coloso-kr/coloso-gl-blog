## 🛠️ Key Skills & Details That Define Professional Quality

![](https://cdn.day1company.io/prod/uploads/202106/183011-380/coloso-hair-kimsanghee-sellingpoint2.png)

This course dives deep into the practical application of cutting techniques, moving beyond abstract theory. You'll learn about:

*   **Body Positioning**: Understanding how your stance and body movement directly influence cutting lines and precision. This is a fundamental skill that affects every aspect of the cut.
*   **Core Cutting Skills**: Mastering essential techniques like one-length cutting, graduation (square and round), and layering. Each is broken down with specific practice methods to solidify your understanding.
*   **Six Disconnection Techniques**: A comprehensive look at practical disconnection styles like Hush Cut, Bob Cut, Leaf Cut, Reverse Layer Tassel Cut, Long Layers, and Short Cut. These are demonstrated with model practice, showing direct salon application.
*   **Head Shape and Texture Adaptation**: Learning how to adapt the ABC+D curriculum specifically for East Asian head shapes and hair textures to enhance volume and create beautiful designs using cutting alone.

These elements combine to provide a robust understanding of women's haircutting that can be immediately applied in a salon environment, ensuring both technical accuracy and client satisfaction.
