## 🛠️ Key Skills & Details That Define Professional Quality

Beyond the core principles, achieving that polished, professional look in anime character art involves a mastery of specific hands-on techniques. These are the details that truly set apart an amateur piece from a work that feels alive and intentional.

*   **Brush Control & Pressure Dynamics:** Understanding how different brushes and pen pressure can create varied line weights and textures is crucial. Experiment with creating soft fades for skin, sharp edges for clothing folds, and dynamic strokes for hair. This isn't just about making lines; it's about conveying form and emotion through line quality.
*   **Workflow Efficiency with Layering:** Professionals utilize layers not just for organization, but as a strategic tool. Learn to group elements logically, use clipping masks effectively for shading, and employ adjustment layers for non-destructive color grading. This streamlines the process, allowing for easier revisions and a cleaner final product.
*   **Intentional Composition:** Every element on the canvas, from the character's gaze to the background elements, should serve a purpose. Chyan's advice highlights using silhouettes, props, and spatial design not just for aesthetics, but to reinforce the character's narrative and the overall mood of the illustration.
