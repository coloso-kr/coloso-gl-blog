## 🛠️ Key Skills & Details That Define Professional Quality

This course delves into practical, hands-on techniques that elevate Blender work from good to groundbreaking. It’s about mastering the workflow for creating entire background scenes that feel lived-in and authentic.

*   **Intentional Modeling**: Understanding how to model components like subway car seats, poles, and windows based on real-world references. This isn't just about shape, but about material and structural integrity.
*   **Custom Texture Creation**: Utilizing vector graphic software to design unique, high-resolution textures. This skill is vital for avoiding generic looks and achieving proprietary aesthetic quality.
*   **Scene Assembly & Lighting**: Combining individual modeled elements into a cohesive scene, paying close attention to scale, arrangement, and the subtle interplay of light and shadow to create depth and atmosphere.
*   **Detailing for Believability**: Adding small, often overlooked details like wear marks, subtle surface imperfections, and environmental effects that contribute to an overall sense of realism.
