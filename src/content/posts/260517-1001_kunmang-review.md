## 💡 3 Core Principles for Rapid Illustration Growth

### 1. Deciphering the Art Lexicon: Beyond Just Knowing Terms
Understanding foundational art terms isn't just about memorizing definitions; it's about building a common language for observation and communication. This course equips you with 149 essential illustration terms, enabling you to dissect and articulate visual elements more effectively. It's not just about learning the technique — it's about shifting how you think about what you're seeing and how to represent it.

### 2. Croquis as a "Speed-Study" Engine
Instead of getting bogged down in detailed rendering early on, Kunmang emphasizes rapid croquis and shape practice. This method, involving exercises like "Planar Croquis Basics for 2x Faster Coloring," trains your brain to quickly analyze forms and volumes from different angles. This allows for significantly faster initial studies and a more robust understanding of structure, directly impacting how efficiently you can tackle coloring and detail later.

### 3. The "Study Brain" Method: Learning Smarter, Not Just Harder
The key to explosive growth isn't just putting in hours, but optimizing *how* you study. Kunmang's "Study Brain" approach, detailed in the personalized study roadmap, focuses on observation, analysis, and efficient reference gathering. This structured routine and self-feedback process help you identify what’s truly improving your skills, ensuring you double your progress even with the same amount of practice.
