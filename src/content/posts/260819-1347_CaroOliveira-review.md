## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How can an Illustrator optimize their Photoshop workstation for efficiency?**

A. An Illustrator can optimize their Photoshop workstation by organizing the UI for a faster drawing and painting experience. This includes creating custom keystrokes on both Photoshop and Wacom tablets, which allows for quicker access to frequently used tools and functions, ultimately saving significant time during the creative process.

**Q. What are the essential art fundamentals an Illustrator should focus on for character design?**

A. An Illustrator should focus on mastering core art fundamentals such as understanding values, color theory, composition, anatomy, and figure drawing. These elements form the essential skills needed not only for character design but for any illustration, providing a solid base for creating original and visually compelling pieces.

**Q. What are some practical tips for an Illustrator to stay motivated and disciplined in their freelance career?**

A. An Illustrator can stay motivated by consistently practicing and applying learned skills to their art, completing exercises, and continuously learning from other artists. Investing in oneself, treating personal artistic development as a valuable asset, and actively seeking opportunities are key to maintaining discipline and growth in a freelance career.
