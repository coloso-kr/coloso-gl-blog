**Q. What specific techniques does this Illustrator use for creating "cozy" scenes?**

A. The focus is on storytelling through warm imagery and everyday moments. Techniques include using perspective to integrate characters with backgrounds and leveraging photo references to ground scenes in reality, making them feel authentic and inviting.
