## 💡 Kareen's Workflow for Captivating Low-Poly Dioramas

### 1. Efficient Modeling: Building Blocks for Charm
It's not just about making shapes; it's about making the *right* shapes for a low-poly style. Kareen emphasizes creating clean, efficient geometry that supports the stylized aesthetic. Think about how each primitive contributes to the overall silhouette and feel. This approach saves time in the long run and makes texturing much smoother.

### 2. The Power of Hand-Painted Textures: Breathing Life into Models
Forget complex PBR workflows for this style. The magic here lies in hand-painted textures. This isn't about photorealism, but about conveying mood and character with color and brushstrokes. Learn to use masking and smart masks to rapidly develop stylized shading and add depth, focusing on how color choices define the scene's atmosphere and draw the viewer's eye.

### 3. Intentional Composition: Telling a Story with Your Scene
A great diorama isn't just a collection of models; it's a curated moment. Kareen guides you through understanding composition principles to guide the viewer's gaze and enhance the narrative. This involves thinking about foreground, midground, and background elements, using color theory to unify the scene, and ensuring every asset serves the overall story you want to tell.
