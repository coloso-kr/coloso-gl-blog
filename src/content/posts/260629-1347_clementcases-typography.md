## 💡 3 Core Principles for Dynamic Type Design with Clément Cases

### 1. Embrace the Eccentric: Beyond Standard Glyphs
It's not just about learning the technique — it's about shifting how you think about letter construction. Clément emphasizes pushing beyond conventional shapes. Start by sketching freely, exploring unusual curves and forms that break from the norm. This experimental mindset is the bedrock of creating truly unique variable typefaces that possess distinct personalities. Don't be afraid to be bold; the most captivating typefaces often stem from the wildest ideas.

### 2. Master Cavalry for Seamless Animation
Cavalry is your secret weapon for bringing static type to life. Its streamlined 2D animation workflow is crucial. Focus on understanding its core motion principles — timing, easing, and keyframing. This isn't about complex 3D rendering; it's about precise control over simple yet effective animated movements that enhance your typography without overwhelming it. Think about how a character naturally unfolds or how a word emphasizes a feeling through subtle shifts.

### 3. The Portfolio Case Study: Context is King
Creating a standout case study is about showing your work in action. Develop your variable font and then demonstrate its application. This means adapting your type for branding mockups, social media animations, or even UI elements. The goal is to prove that your original typeface isn't just a digital artifact; it's a versatile design solution that works across various media. This structured approach, moving from concept to application, is what makes your portfolio ready for the real world.

## 🛠️ Key Skills & Details That Define Professional Quality

![](https://cdn.day1company.io/prod/uploads/202412/115713-1093/portfolio-graphicdesigner-clementcases-us-01-02.webp)

To elevate your type design beyond basic lettering, focus on the granular details that Clément Cases highlights. This includes mastering the **workflow for creating variable fonts** within Glyphs, understanding how to define axes and interpolate weights and styles effectively. This technical proficiency is paramount for ensuring your custom typefaces are not only visually striking but also functionally robust.

Beyond static design, the art of **kinetic typography** hinges on understanding subtle motion principles. This involves learning to animate your letterforms using software like Cavalry, focusing on expressive transitions and dynamic layouts that complement the typeface's inherent personality. It’s about making each letter and word contribute to a larger visual narrative.

Finally, the ability to **apply your original typography across diverse formats** is what truly sets a designer apart. This means understanding how to package your font files for various uses – from web embedding to print branding – and how to create compelling animated sequences that showcase your type's unique characteristics for platforms like social media. This holistic approach ensures your designs are not just aesthetically pleasing but also commercially viable and portfolio-ready.
